Is Clear Glass Safe?

Final project · Stage 1 proposal

Context

I visited all 17 toilets of THE TOKYO TOILET. Shigeru Ban’s glass toilets are clear when empty and turn frosted when locked.

Question

Does seeing inside first really make people feel safer?

Method

A phone 3D survey with three.js. Four walls: solid, frosted, frosted after locking, clear. People rate before entering and after sitting down.

Output

Google Apps Script saves the answers as JSON to Google Sheets: a dataset comparing privacy and safety.
