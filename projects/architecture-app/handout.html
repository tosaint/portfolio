My Website

Portfolio / Website CV · tosaint.com

Idea

My website is built on one line from Zhuangzi: when a space is clear, there is room for good things to stay. So the main idea is to leave space open.

Home and works

The home page comes from my wood work, Water’s Echo in Wood. On a paper-white background, soft rings spread like ripples when you touch the screen or move your mouse. Each work first appears as a black-and-white line drawing; its own page shows the real colors and materials.

Working with AI

I built the site with AI. I led the layout, the order of the content and the interactions, and gave the AI photos and screenshots. I kept checking and changing the results. A frame like a building drawing felt too limiting, so I removed it and kept open space and simple lines.

How it works

Content is separate from design. profile.json and projects.json hold the content, and JavaScript builds the work cards, the index and my CV. To add a work, I only edit JSON, not HTML. Git saves every version, and GitHub Pages puts the site online at tosaint.com.

Is Clear Glass Safe?

Final project · Stage 1 concept proposal

Context

This summer I visited all 17 toilets of THE TOKYO TOILET in Shibuya. Shigeru Ban’s transparent toilet was the most interesting: you can see in when it is empty, and the glass turns cloudy when you lock the door.

Problem

I was still afraid to use it. A 2026 floor-plan study also found these toilets have the lowest privacy score, but a plan cannot show how people feel. Do people really feel safer if they can see in first?

Users and current workflow

Architects and researchers test designs with photo surveys or site interviews. Photos cannot show the glass changing, and on site you cannot control the view or the people walking by.

Intervention

So I built a phone 3D survey with three.js and AI. People choose a toilet and try four walls: opaque, frosted, frosted after locking, and clear, in a balanced order with the same passers-by. They rate before entering and again from memory after locking. “I can’t say” is saved separately.

Given → performs → produces

Given ratings for four wall types, the system controls the order, matches each person’s answers and saves them as JSON in Google Sheets, producing a dataset that compares privacy and safety.

MVP and questions

One toilet, four walls, complete data saving. Next: is the phone close to the real place, and how many people does the study need?
