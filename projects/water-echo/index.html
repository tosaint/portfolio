Work 001 · Plywood · 2008

水聲木

Water’s Echo in Wood

2008 臺灣國際木雕競賽 入選Selected, 2008 Taiwan International Wood Sculpture Competition

水聲落下，漣漪漾開，
尋找心中剎那的感動，
暫存當下的共鳴。

A sound of water, ripples unfolding —
seeking a fleeting stir within,
holding, for a moment, its resonance.
