關於

2002 年，高師大工業科技教育學系大三的「木器製造」課，在呂書榮老師指導下，我照《營造法式》的圖樣自己打樣、裁料、開口，做了這組斗栱。每一件靠開口互相咬住，拆得開也裝得回去。

模型後來不在了，只剩一組小小的照片，也沒有比例尺。這次的復原完全從照片判讀：每一層有哪些構件、多長、怎麼咬合，都對著照片一件件確認；當年的做法和法式不同的地方，照原作做，再另外註記。

About

In 2002, in the Wood Products Manufacturing course of my third year in Industrial Technology Education at National Kaohsiung Normal University, taught by 呂書榮, I set out, cut and notched this bracket set from the drawings of the Yingzao Fashi. Every piece locks into the next by its notches, so it comes apart and goes back together.

The model itself is gone; what is left is a set of small photographs with no scale in them. This rebuild is read entirely from those photos: which pieces sit on each tier, how long they are and how they lock were checked against the photos one by one. Where the 2002 model differs from the Fashi, the rebuild follows the original and notes the difference.

材分制，和五鋪作怎麼數

《營造法式》不直接寫尺寸，而是先定一個「材」：一根栱的斷面，高 15 分、寬 10 分。上下兩層栱之間的空隙叫「栔」，高 6 分，由斗墊滿。材加栔是 21 分，就是一層的高度；每往外出一跳是 30 分。建築等級高，就把一分放大；等級低，就縮小。

「鋪作」數的是一朵斗栱疊了幾層：櫨斗、華栱、昂、耍頭、襯方頭，共五層，所以是五鋪作。往外出了兩跳，一跳是華栱（杪），一跳是昂，叫「單杪單昂」。第一跳上的橫栱疊兩層（瓜子栱上加慢栱），叫「重栱」；每一跳都有橫栱，叫「計心」。

The modular unit, and counting five tiers

The Yingzao Fashi does not give sizes directly. It first fixes the cai: the cross-section of one bracket arm, 15 fen high and 10 fen wide. The gap between one tier of arms and the next is the qi, 6 fen high and filled by a block. Cai plus qi is 21 fen, the height of one tier; each step outward is 30 fen. A grander building simply uses a larger fen.

Puzuo counts the tiers of one bracket set: the main block, the projecting arm, the ang, the nose piece and the top tie — five, so this is a five-tier set. It reaches out twice, once with a projecting arm and once with an ang. The crosswise arms on the first step are doubled, and every step carries crosswise arms.

和法式不一樣的地方

宋式的昂是斜的：一根長料從外往內斜斜穿過好幾層，外端的昂嘴往下垂，內端的昂尾往上壓在槫下，像槓桿一樣平衡屋簷。當年的模型把昂做成水平的一層，只在外端削出昂嘴，比較接近明清以後的「假昂」。

裡外也因此不對稱。昂的內端很短，不承斗，裡側的第二跳改由耍頭的尾端承一個斗，所以裡側的令栱和上面的方，都比外側高一層。

昂和襯方頭的內端做成雲形，耍頭外端斜切、內端削尖，槫用真的竹子。這些都照原作保留。

Where it differs from the Fashi

A Song ang is sloped: one long timber runs down and out through several tiers, its beak dipping at the eave and its tail rising inside to press under a purlin, balancing the eave like a lever. The 2002 model makes the ang a level tier with a beak cut on its outer end, closer to the false ang of later Ming and Qing work.

That also makes the two sides unequal. The inner end of the ang is short and carries nothing, so the second step inside rests on a block seated on the tail of the nose piece, and the inner top arm and the beam above it sit one tier higher than their outer counterparts.

The cloud-shaped inner ends of the ang and the top tie, the nose piece cut on a slant outside and pointed inside, and the real bamboo purlins all follow the original.
