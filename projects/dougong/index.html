關於

2002 年，高師大工業科技教育學系大三的「木器製造」課，在呂書榮老師指導下，我照《營造法式》的圖樣自己打樣、裁料、開口，做了這組斗栱。沒有釘子也沒有膠，每一件靠開口互相咬住，拆得開也裝得回去。

模型後來不在了，只剩一組小小的照片，也沒有比例尺。這次的復原以照片判讀為主，看不清楚的地方套回法式的標準尺寸；當年理解和法式不同的地方，照原作做，再另外註記。

About

In 2002, in the Wood Products Manufacturing course of my third year in Industrial Technology Education at National Kaohsiung Normal University, under Prof. Lu Shu-Jung, I set out, cut and notched this bracket set from the drawings of the Yingzao Fashi. No nails and no glue: every piece locks into the next by its notches, so it comes apart and goes back together.

The model itself is gone; what is left is a set of small photographs with no scale in them. This rebuild reads the proportions from the photos and falls back on the standard dimensions of the Fashi where the photos are unclear. Where my reading in 2002 differs from the Fashi, the rebuild follows the original and notes the difference.

材分制

《營造法式》不直接寫尺寸，而是先定一個「材」：一根栱的斷面，高 15 分、寬 10 分。上下兩層栱之間的空隙叫「栔」，高 6 分。材加栔是「足材」，21 分。

所有構件都用分來量：栱多長、斗多大、每一跳出去多遠。建築等級高，就把一分放大；等級低，就縮小。這個 3D 模型也只用分來建，所以畫面上沒有公分。

The modular unit

The Yingzao Fashi does not give sizes directly. It first fixes the cai: the cross-section of one bracket arm, 15 fen high and 10 fen wide. The gap between one tier of arms and the next is the qi, 6 fen high. Cai plus qi makes a full cai of 21 fen.

Everything is measured in fen: how long an arm is, how big a block is, how far each step reaches out. A grander building simply uses a larger fen. This model is built in fen alone, so there are no centimetres on screen.

五鋪作怎麼數

「鋪作」數的是一朵斗栱疊了幾層：櫨斗一、華栱一、昂一、耍頭一、襯方頭一，共五層，所以是五鋪作。往外出了兩跳，一跳是華栱（杪），一跳是昂，叫「單杪單昂」。橫向的栱在跳頭疊兩層（瓜子栱上加慢栱），叫「重栱」；每一跳都有橫栱，叫「計心」。

Counting five tiers

Puzuo counts the tiers of one bracket set: the main block, the projecting arm, the ang, the nose piece and the piece above it — five, so this is a five-tier set. It reaches out twice, once with a projecting arm and once with an ang: one arm, one ang. The crosswise arms are doubled at each step (a short arm with a long one on top), and every step carries crosswise arms.

和法式不一樣的地方

宋式的昂是斜的：一根長料從外往內斜斜穿過好幾層，外端的昂嘴往下垂，內端的昂尾往上壓在槫下，像槓桿一樣平衡屋簷。當年的模型把昂做成水平的一層，只在外端削出昂嘴，比較接近明清以後的「假昂」。

昂和襯方頭的內端做成雲形，耍頭兩頭都削尖，槫用真的竹子。這些都照原作保留。

令栱是否裡外都有、內側深色方與外側竹槫的確切位置，照片看不清楚，當年的細節也已不記得，目前依照片判讀。

Where it differs from the Fashi

A Song ang is sloped: one long timber runs down and out through several tiers, its beak dipping at the eave and its tail rising inside to press under a purlin, balancing the eave like a lever. The 2002 model makes the ang a level tier with a beak cut on its outer end, closer to the false ang of later Ming and Qing work.

The cloud-shaped inner ends of the ang and the top piece, the nose pieces pointed at both ends, and the real bamboo purlins all follow the original.

Whether the top arms sit on both sides, and exactly where the inner dark beam and the outer bamboo purlin lie, cannot be seen clearly in the photos and are no longer remembered; they follow my best reading of the photos.
