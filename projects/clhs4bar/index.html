About

A bronze horse from the former Zhongli Shrine still stands on the school campus. This paper one lets it run across a desk: one sheet of card, four thumbtacks and a piece of foam board. Turn the disc and the horse steps out.

The bronze horse has been moved three times on campus and now faces the school gate.

It is the worked example for the mechanism unit in Technology. Change the bar lengths in the simulator on this page first and see how the gait changes and when it jams; then print the worksheet, draw your own figure on the 1 cm grid, cut it out and put it together.

四根桿、四個軸

四連桿機構是四根桿子用四個軸首尾相接成一圈。把其中一根固定住，轉動最短的那根，另外兩根就被帶著動。

壢馬把四根桿子藏在身體裡。底座是固定桿，圓盤是曲柄，前半身是連桿，後半身是搖桿。桿長指的是兩個孔之間的距離，和零件的外形無關。

Four bars, four pivots

A four-bar linkage is four bars joined end to end by four pivots into a loop. Hold one bar still, turn the shortest, and the other two are carried along.

The horse hides the four bars in its body. The base is the fixed bar, the disc is the crank, the front half is the coupler and the back half is the rocker. A bar's length is the distance between its two holes; the outline of the part does not matter.

為什麼轉得動

曲柄要能轉一整圈，有兩個條件：最短的桿加最長的桿，不能比另外兩根加起來還長；而且最短的那根要是曲柄。壢馬是 1 + 11 ≤ 8 + 11，曲柄 1 cm 也是最短的，所以轉得動。

剛好相等的時候叫做臨界：桿子會拉成一直線。數學上接得起來，實體卻會因為摩擦和孔位誤差卡住，所以實作要留餘裕。把兩個固定軸拉遠或靠近，看看三種狀態。

Why it turns

The crank can go all the way round on two conditions: the shortest bar plus the longest must not be longer than the other two together, and the shortest bar must be the crank. For the horse that is 1 + 11 ≤ 8 + 11 with a 1 cm crank, so it turns.

When the two sides are exactly equal the linkage is critical: the bars line up straight. It closes on paper, but friction and small hole errors make a real one jam, so leave a margin. Move the fixed pivots apart or together and watch the three states.

從骨架到造型

機構決定怎麼動，輪廓決定看起來像什麼。同一副骨架，孔位不變，外形可以換成任何東西。

馬身上每一點走的路都不一樣：圓盤上的點繞圓，後半身的點來回畫弧，前半身的點走出一條封閉的曲線。設計造型時，先想清楚哪個部位要怎麼動。下面的模擬器可以關掉輪廓只看骨架，點馬身上任何一點看它的軌跡。

From skeleton to shape

The linkage decides how it moves; the outline decides what it looks like. Keep the same skeleton and the same holes, and the shape can become anything.

Every point on the horse takes its own path: points on the disc go round a circle, points on the back half swing along an arc, and points on the front half trace a closed curve. When you design a figure, decide first which part should move in which way. In the simulator below you can hide the outline to see the bare skeleton, and click any point on the horse to see its path.

    學習單 · Worksheet

    一張 A4 橫向、1:1，雙面列印後對折成 A5。One A4 sheet, 1:1, printed on both sides and folded to A5.

    下載標準版（11 / 8 / 11 / 1）

    列印時紙張選 A4 橫向、縮放 100%（實際大小）、邊界無；雙面列印選短邊翻面。上方 4 公分正反面都不放零件，裁切前先留下來。

    動手做

    材料：紙卡（厚 1–2 mm）、圖釘 4 支、珍珠板、剪刀、口紅膠。

    1. 在學習單內頁的格子上畫出自己的造型，孔位不動，交給老師確認。
    2. 沿虛線裁下上方的保留帶留著，把零件頁貼到紙卡上，或描到紙卡上。
    3. 沿輪廓剪下前半身、後半身、底座和圓盤。
    4. 圖釘對準十字記號，在每個零件上戳出孔。
    5. 底座固定在珍珠板上，照 A、B、C、D 用圖釘把零件串起來，轉動圓盤。

    Make one

    You need card 1–2 mm thick, four thumbtacks, foam board, scissors and a glue stick.

    1. Draw your own figure on the grid inside the worksheet. Leave the holes where they are, and have the teacher check it.
    2. Cut off the top strip along the dashed line and keep it. Glue the parts page onto card, or trace it across.
    3. Cut out the front half, the back half, the base and the disc.
    4. Push a thumbtack through each cross to make the holes.
    5. Fix the base to the foam board, pin the parts together at A, B, C and D, and turn the disc.

    進階：用馬達帶動

    下一步是用 TT 減速馬達取代轉圓盤的手。馬達可以放在板子前面，也可以藏在板子後面；兩種做法的高度和固定方式不同，之後會加進這一頁的模擬器。

    Next: drive it with a motor

    The next step replaces the hand on the disc with a TT gear motor. The motor can sit in front of the board or hide behind it; the two need different heights and fixings, and both will be added to the simulator.
