仿生獸

Beast

一個曲柄，帶著兩組四連桿和一個平行四邊形，讓腳掌畫出一圈像走路的軌跡。One crank drives two four-bar linkages and a parallelogram, and the foot traces a loop like a step.

 看學習單 ↓Worksheet · A4 · 1:1

先做一隻腳：7 片零件加一條底座。想做兩隻腳，同一張再剪一份、翻面就是另一隻。

關於

名字借自荷蘭藝術家 Theo Jansen 的「仿生獸」。他的一隻腳有十多根桿；這一隻簡單得多：兩組四連桿加一個平行四邊形，全部由中間一個曲柄帶動。

每個軸孔都落在 1 公分的格點上，連斜的桿也是（√34、√61），所以學習單不用轉角度，照格子就能畫。孔位代號沿用 MotionGen 的 J1 到 J14。

About

The name borrows from Theo Jansen's Strandbeest. One of his legs has more than a dozen bars; this one is much simpler: two four-bar linkages and a parallelogram, all driven by one crank in the middle.

Every pivot sits on a point of the 1 cm grid, even on the slanted bars (√34 and √61), so the worksheet needs no angles: just follow the grid. The holes keep their MotionGen names, J1 to J14.

拆開來看

一隻腳裡藏著三個四邊形。上下兩組四連桿共用曲柄 J1–J4 和固定桿 J1–J2，一個推上三角板，一個推直桿；平行四邊形再把兩者的動作合在一起，交給腳掌。

點右邊的按鈕，看每一組是哪四個孔。

Take it apart

One leg hides three four-sided loops. The upper and lower four-bars share the crank J1–J4 and the fixed bar J1–J2; one drives the upper triangle, the other the straight bar. The parallelogram then combines the two and passes them to the foot.

Use the buttons on the right to see which four holes make each loop.

學習單 · Worksheet

一張 A4 橫向、1:1，雙面列印後對折成 A5。印的是一隻腳。One A4 sheet, 1:1, double-sided and folded to A5. It holds one leg.

列印時紙張選 A4 橫向、縮放 100%（實際大小）、邊界無；雙面列印選短邊翻面。上方 4 公分正反面都不放零件，裁切前先留下來。

動手做：一隻腳

材料：紙卡（厚 1–2 mm）、圖釘 7 支、珍珠板、剪刀、口紅膠。

  1. 在內頁格子上畫兩塊三角板的造型，孔位不動，交給老師確認。
  2. 沿虛線裁下上方的保留帶留著，把零件頁貼到紙卡上，或描到紙卡上，剪下 7 片零件和底座。
  3. 底座用 J1、J2 兩支圖釘釘在珍珠板上。
  4. 照下面的順序由下往上疊，同名的孔對齊，用圖釘串起來：
  • 底座
  • 圓盤
  • L4
  • L3
  • L8
  • L7
  • L9
  • L10

這個順序讓每支圖釘都在最上面那片之上，斜桿 L7、L8 不會被圖釘頭擋住。

Make one leg

You need card 1–2 mm thick, seven thumbtacks, foam board, scissors and a glue stick.

  1. Draw your own shapes for the two triangles on the grid inside. Leave the holes where they are and have the teacher check it.
  2. Cut off the top strip and keep it. Glue the parts page onto card, or trace it, and cut out the seven parts and the base.
  3. Pin the base to the foam board at J1 and J2.
  4. Stack the parts from the bottom up in the order shown, matching holes with the same name, and pin them together.

In this order every thumbtack sits on top of the highest part it holds, so the slanted bars L7 and L8 clear the tack heads.

兩隻腳

同一張學習單再剪一份，整份翻面就是左腳。孔位代號換成：J2→J3、J5→J8、J6→J9、J7→J10、J11→J13、J12→J14。底座多釘一個 J3，兩隻腳共用同一個圓盤和 J4。

J4 上會疊 5 層：圓盤加 L7、L8、L11、L12，一般圖釘的針可能不夠長，換長一點的圖釘或大頭針。全部共 13 片零件、12 支圖釘。

進階：讓左右腳差半圈（180°），一隻抬起時另一隻剛好著地。這需要兩個曲柄銷，可以當成自己設計的挑戰。

Two legs

Cut a second copy of the same sheet and turn the whole set over for the left leg. The holes become J2→J3, J5→J8, J6→J9, J7→J10, J11→J13 and J12→J14. Pin the base at J3 as well; both legs share the disc and J4.

J4 then holds five layers: the disc plus L7, L8, L11 and L12. An ordinary thumbtack may be too short, so use a longer pin. That makes 13 parts and 12 pins in all.

Going further: put the legs half a turn (180°) apart so one foot lifts as the other lands. It needs two crank pins, so treat it as a design challenge of your own.
