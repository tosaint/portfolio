Dog

一個曲柄、四片會動的零件，四隻腳輪流走、頭上下點，尾巴跟著後腳搖。One crank and four moving parts: the legs step in turn, the head nods and the tail wags with the hind leg.

6 片零件：底座、曲柄圓盤，和狗的四片身體。

關於

這隻狗的外形和孔位照 MotionGen 的設計 1:1 搬過來。只有一個曲柄在轉，四片零件卻各走各的：頭和一隻前腳是同一片，身體連著一隻後腳，另一隻前腳單獨一片，後腳和尾巴是同一片。

真正要釘圖釘的只有 5 個孔：底座上的 J1、J2，和活動的 J3、J4、J6。腳尖和頭頂只是看軌跡用的點，不用打孔。

About

The dog's shape and holes come 1:1 from a MotionGen design. Only one crank turns, yet the four parts each move their own way: the head shares a part with one front leg, the body carries a hind leg, the other front leg is its own part, and the hind leg carries the tail.

Only five holes take a tack: J1 and J2 on the base, and the moving J3, J4 and J6. The tips of the feet and the head are just points to watch.

孔不在格點上

這次孔位照原設計的小數，例如 J3 在 (−2.503, −0.454)。學習單照 1:1 精確印，剪的時候沿線剪、孔照十字打，動作就和模擬一樣。

Holes off the grid

The holes keep the design's decimals, for example J3 at (−2.503, −0.454). The sheet prints them exactly at 1:1, so cut on the line and pierce at the cross and it moves like the simulation.

學習單 · Worksheet

一張 A4 橫向、1:1，雙面列印後對折成 A5。One A4 sheet, 1:1, printed on both sides and folded to A5.

列印時紙張選 A4 橫向、縮放 100%（實際大小）、邊界無；雙面列印選短邊翻面。上方 4 公分正反面都不放零件，裁切前先留下來。

動手做

材料：紙卡（厚 1–2 mm）、圖釘 5 支、珍珠板、剪刀、口紅膠。

  1. 在內頁格子上畫自己的造型，孔位不動，交給老師確認。
  2. 沿虛線裁下上方的保留帶留著，把零件頁貼到紙卡上，或描到紙卡上，剪下所有零件。
  3. 底座用固定孔（實心）的圖釘釘在珍珠板上。
  4. 同名的孔疊在一起，用圖釘串起來，順時鐘轉圓盤。

Make one

You need card, five thumbtacks, foam board, scissors and a glue stick.

  1. Draw your own shapes on the grid inside. Leave the holes where they are and have the teacher check it.
  2. Cut off the top strip and keep it. Glue the parts page onto card, or trace it, and cut out every part.
  3. Pin the base to the foam board through the fixed (solid) holes.
  4. Stack holes with the same name, pin them together and turn the disc clockwise.
