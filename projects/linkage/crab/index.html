Crab

 看學習單 ↓Worksheet · A4 · 1:1

10 片零件：殼（底座）、曲柄圓盤、左右連桿、左右搖桿、左右眼柄、左右大螯。

關於

殼就是底座，上面有 5 個固定孔：曲柄中心 J1、兩根眼柄的軸 J2、J3，和兩根搖桿的軸 J4、J5。曲柄銷 J6 同時推左右兩根連桿，每一邊都是一組六連桿：連桿、搖桿和眼柄一起決定大螯的位置。

這隻螃蟹照 MotionGen 的設計 1:1 搬過來，零件不用縮小就放得進一張 A4。

About

The shell is the base, with five fixed holes: J1 at the crank centre, J2 and J3 for the two eye stalks, and J4 and J5 for the two rockers. The crank pin J6 pushes the left and right rods at once, and each side is a six-bar linkage: the rod, the rocker and the eye stalk together set where the claw goes.

This crab comes 1:1 from a MotionGen design, and the parts fit on one A4 sheet without scaling.

左右不一樣大

兩隻大螯看起來對稱，其實擺動的幅度不一樣：左螯左右約 8.2 cm，右螯約 7.1 cm。因為殼上的兩個搖桿軸 J4、J5 高度差了約 0.5 cm，左右兩組的形狀就不完全一樣。

曲柄 2.065 cm 已經很接近上限（約 2.1 cm），孔要照十字打準，轉起來才順。

Not quite symmetric

The claws look symmetric but swing by different amounts: about 8.2 cm on the left and 7.1 cm on the right. The rocker pivots J4 and J5 sit about 0.5 cm apart in height, so the two sides are not exact mirror images.

The 2.065 cm crank is close to its limit of about 2.1 cm, so pierce each hole right on its cross for a smooth turn.

學習單 · Worksheet

一張 A4 橫向、1:1，雙面列印後對折成 A5。One A4 sheet, 1:1, printed on both sides and folded to A5.

列印時紙張選 A4 橫向、縮放 100%（實際大小）、邊界無；雙面列印選短邊翻面。上方 4 公分正反面都不放零件，裁切前先留下來。

動手做

材料：紙卡（厚 1–2 mm）、圖釘 12 支、珍珠板、剪刀、口紅膠。

  1. 在內頁格子上畫自己的造型，孔位不動，交給老師確認。
  2. 沿虛線裁下上方的保留帶留著，把零件頁貼到紙卡上，或描到紙卡上，剪下所有零件。
  3. 底座用固定孔（實心）的圖釘釘在珍珠板上。
  4. 同名的孔疊在一起，用圖釘串起來，順時鐘轉圓盤。

Make one

You need card, twelve thumbtacks, foam board, scissors and a glue stick.

  1. Draw your own shapes on the grid inside. Leave the holes where they are and have the teacher check it.
  2. Cut off the top strip and keep it. Glue the parts page onto card, or trace it, and cut out every part.
  3. Pin the base to the foam board through the fixed (solid) holes.
  4. Stack holes with the same name, pin them together and turn the disc clockwise.
