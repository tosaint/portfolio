7 片零件，大臂上有一條滑槽要剪出來。

關於

這個範例多了一種接法：滑槽。曲柄銷 J4 不是釘在大臂上，而是插在大臂的長槽裡。曲柄轉一圈，銷在槽裡來回滑，大臂就來回擺；這叫做擺動導桿機構。

大臂再經過小臂、推桿和拉桿兩組四連桿，把擺動傳到挖斗，挖斗尖端 J14 畫出挖土的軌跡。

About

This example adds a new kind of joint: a slot. The crank pin J4 is not pinned to the boom but rides in a long slot in it. As the crank turns, the pin slides back and forth and the boom swings; this is a slotted-lever mechanism.

Two more four-bars, through the arm, the push rod and the pull rod, carry the swing to the bucket, whose tip J14 traces the digging path.

滑槽要多長

銷在槽裡滑的距離，剛好是曲柄長的兩倍。槽太短，銷會頂到槽的兩端卡住。

MotionGen 的 PDF 沒有記錄滑槽，這裡的滑槽位置是從 J3–J2 這條線推算的，長度照 SVG 原稿。

How long a slot

The pin slides exactly twice the crank length. If the slot is too short, the pin hits its ends and jams.

The MotionGen PDF does not record the slot; its line here is worked out from J3–J2, and its length follows the SVG.

學習單 · Worksheet

一張 A4 橫向、1:1，雙面列印後對折成 A5。One A4 sheet, 1:1, printed on both sides and folded to A5.

列印時紙張選 A4 橫向、縮放 100%（實際大小）、邊界無；雙面列印選短邊翻面。上方 4 公分正反面都不放零件，裁切前先留下來。

動手做

材料：紙卡（厚 1–2 mm）、圖釘 8 支、珍珠板、剪刀、美工刀、口紅膠。

  1. 在內頁格子上畫自己的造型，孔位不動，交給老師確認。
  2. 沿虛線裁下上方的保留帶留著，把零件頁貼到紙卡上，或描到紙卡上，剪下所有零件。
  3. 底座用固定孔（實心）的圖釘釘在珍珠板上。
  4. 同名的孔疊在一起，用圖釘串起來，順時鐘轉圓盤。

大臂的滑槽要用美工刀割出來，寬度比圖釘的針大一點，銷才滑得動。

Make one

You need card, eight thumbtacks, foam board, scissors, a craft knife and a glue stick.

  1. Draw your own shapes on the grid inside. Leave the holes where they are and have the teacher check it.
  2. Cut off the top strip and keep it. Glue the parts page onto card, or trace it, and cut out every part.
  3. Pin the base to the foam board through the fixed (solid) holes.
  4. Stack holes with the same name, pin them together and turn the disc clockwise.
