Scroll down and the plate on the right follows each paragraph through the motor, the gear train, the axles and the case, then a 3D simulator, an Onshape guide to modelling the case and two engineering drawings.

07 · In 3D3D 模擬與外殼建模

拖曳旋轉，切換實體、透視、爆炸，調整電壓與負載；切到「外殼建模」，跟著八個步驟在 Onshape 畫出外殼。Drag to orbit, switch solid, x-ray and exploded views, set voltage and load. Switch to the case tab to follow eight steps for modelling the case in Onshape.

08 · Drawings工程圖

第三角法，比例 2:1，單位 mm。第 1 張為外觀圖，第 2 張為外殼零件圖；剖面 A–A 中上下殼以相反方向的剖面線區分。括號內為參考尺寸。Third-angle projection, scale 2:1, millimetres. Sheet 1 shows the exterior, sheet 2 the case; in section A–A the two shells are hatched in opposite directions. Bracketed values are for reference.

Note · 說明

Outer dimensions are measured from a real motor; interior details are estimated from teardown photographs and may differ between makers. Motor figures follow Adafruit’s published measurements at 3–6 V.
